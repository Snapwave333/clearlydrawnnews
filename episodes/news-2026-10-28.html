STUDIOSWreck-It Ralph Ride Coming to Tokyo Disneyland

Tokyo Disneyland is getting a brand new Wreck-It Ralph ride. Japanese outlet Anime Anime reports it opens April 15, 2027. The ride is called Sugar Rush Sweet Rescue. It's coming to the Tomorrowland area of the park. It's the world's very first ride based on this movie.

Source: Anime!Anime! (animeanime.jp), translated from Japanese

ANIMEMashle Season 3 Arrives in January 2027

Mashle: Magic and Muscles is coming back for season three. Crunchyroll News reports it starts streaming in January 2027. In this story, everyone uses magic, but one young man can't. This new season is called the Tri-Magicathalon Divine Visionary Final Exam Arc. Crunchyroll will stream it around the world, except in Japan and China.

Official trailer from Crunchyroll on YouTube
Source: Crunchyroll News (crunchyroll.com/news) · Anime News Network (animenewsnetwork.com)

ANIMECardfight!! Vanguard Announces New Anime

Cardfight Vanguard has a brand new television anime called Veil Lift. Anime News Network reports the franchise's official account shared an announcement video on Wednesday. More details arrive on December 7. Earlier, the franchise teased that a completely new television anime will air in 2027. That's for its fifteenth anniversary.

Source: Anime News Network (animenewsnetwork.com)

GAMESGundam Battle Operation 3 Coming to PS5, PC

Bandai Namco just announced Mobile Suit Gundam Battle Operation 3. It's coming to PlayStation 5, and to computers through Steam. Anime News Network and Crunchyroll News report it was revealed on Wednesday. That happened during the Gundam Games fortieth Anniversary livestream, with a trailer previewing the gameplay. It's a free-to-play team game with an expanded Mobile Suit roster.

Official trailer from Bandai Namco Entertainment America on YouTube
Source: Anime News Network (animenewsnetwork.com) · Crunchyroll News (crunchyroll.com/news)

CARTOONSPlay-Doh Gets a Weekly Animated Kids Show

Play-Doh is getting its very own animated show! Hasbro has announced Play-Doh with Margo, the brand's first original YouTube series. Animation World Network reports the official trailer is here. In each weekly preschool episode, host Margo shapes a creation from Play-Doh compound. Then that creation comes to life in an animated short story.

Official trailer from Play-Doh Official on YouTube
Source: Animation World Network (awn.com)

ANIMEBAND-MAID Shares Beyblade X Ending Video

Crunchyroll News reports that rock band Band-Maid just shared a music video for Spin, the new ending theme for Beyblade X. The single Spin came out in Japan on October 28, 2026. The music video went up that same day, on the band's official YouTube channel. It's the ending theme for the Bey Kingdom Arc. That's season four of the Beyblade X anime, which premiered on October 9.

Source: Crunchyroll News (crunchyroll.com/news)

GAMESCozy Game TANUKI: Pon's Summer Out Nov. 10

A cozy game about a tanuki who delivers the mail is almost here! Tanuki, Pon's Summer launches on November 10. Anime News Network reports the news came from Nintendo's Indie World Showcase. It comes from Denkiworks, an indie studio based in Kyoto. They shared the release date on Wednesday, during the showcase livestream.

Official trailer from CRITICAL REFLEX on YouTube
Source: Anime News Network (animenewsnetwork.com)

MOVIESLaika's Wildwood Opens With $12.2 Million

Travis Knight's stop-motion film Wildwood, from studio Laika, opened with twelve point two million dollars. Cartoon Brew reports it beat many careful predictions. That was the weekend of October 23 to twenty-fifth. Wildwood landed in third place. It played in two thousand nine hundred forty-two theaters, according to distributor Fathom Entertainment.

Source: Cartoon Brew (cartoonbrew.com)

ANIMEPrecure Gets Villain-Heroine Spinoff Anime

Toei Animation just announced a brand new Precure spinoff TV anime. It's called Dual Shadow, Precure Villain Heroines, and it premieres next year. Anime News Network reports this one is a little different. Unlike most earlier Precure spinoffs, it will have an original story and brand new characters. Precure has a tradition of villains becoming heroes.
