MOVIESCoyote vs. Acme Enters Oscar Race

Coyote versus Acme can now compete for the animated feature Oscar. Cartoon Brew reports the Academy ruled the film eligible, citing Gold Derby. It's Dave Green's mix of live action and animation, written by Samy Burch. Coyote and a human lawyer, played by Will Forte, sue the company whose products keep failing him. Warner Brothers shelved the finished film in 2023.

Source: Cartoon Brew (cartoonbrew.com)

GAMESFortnite Teases One Piece Crossover

The Straw Hats from One Piece are sailing into Fortnite. Crunchyroll News reports the event is called Fortnite Treasure Hunters. Epic Games just shared the first teaser trailer. It gives a quick peek at some One Piece character skins. The event launches on November 1.

Source: Crunchyroll News (crunchyroll.com/news)

RETROCowboy Bebop Film Gets 4K Remaster

Cowboy Bebop: The Movie is getting a shiny new four K remaster. Crunchyroll News reports a teaser trailer for the remastered footage arrived today. The film turns twenty-five this year. To celebrate, theaters in Japan will hold four K remastered screenings, starting on October 30. In Japan, the movie has a different name.

Source: Crunchyroll News (crunchyroll.com/news)

ANIMEIruma-kun Spinoff Anime Premieres Jan 4

Iruma-kun is getting a spinoff anime. Anime News Network reports IruMafia Edition premieres January 4. The official website opened on Tuesday. It showed off the main visual and confirmed a twelve-episode run. The show airs on NHK General at eleven at night, Japan time.

Source: Anime News Network (animenewsnetwork.com) · Crunchyroll News (crunchyroll.com/news)

ANIMEDetective Conan Collections Stream on Netflix

A fourth collection of Detective Conan episodes arrives November 1. Anime News Network reports it's called Clash of the Red and Black. It streams on both Crunchyroll and Netflix. These curated collections come in Japanese, and with a brand-new English dub by Studio Nano. It all started in July 2025.

Source: Anime News Network (animenewsnetwork.com)

ANIMEPhantom Busters Anime Reveals Main Cast

Phantom Busters is getting a TV anime. Anime News Network reports a teaser picture, the main cast, and the main staff were all revealed on Monday. Chika Nagaoka is directing the anime at Toho Animation Studio. Takahiro Kishida, who worked on Haikyu, and Rina Iwamoto are designing the characters. Straight A student Eugene Korekishi doesn't believe in ghosts.

Source: Anime News Network (animenewsnetwork.com) · Crunchyroll News (crunchyroll.com/news)

ANIMEShaman King Creator Designs Mecha Samurai

Hiroyuki Takei, the creator of Shaman King, drafted the main character designs for a brand new short anime called Mecha Samurai. Anime News Network reports that TV Tokyo is teaming up with Plott, a company that makes short web anime. It's TV Tokyo's first anime built for streaming and social media. The story is set in present-day Japan. Mechanical ghosts arrive through a Demon Gate from another dimension.

Source: Anime News Network (animenewsnetwork.com)

MOVIESGKIDS Releases cocoon Anime on Digital

A moving anime has just arrived on your screens at home. Anime News Network reports that GKIDS released cocoon, One Summer of Girlhood, digitally on Apple TV and Prime Video. Prefer something you can hold in your hands? GKIDS will also release it on Blu-ray Disc on January 19. The anime is based on Machiko Kyō's manga.

Source: Anime News Network (animenewsnetwork.com)
