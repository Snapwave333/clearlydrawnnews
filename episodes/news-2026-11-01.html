MOVIESLaika Fantasy Eyes $7-8M Second Weekend

Laika's stop-motion fantasy Wildwood has passed twenty million dollars at the domestic box office. That's according to Cartoon Brew. Wildwood is adding screens. That means even more movie screens are showing it, so more families might spot it at their local theater. Now, what about weekend number two?

Source: Cartoon Brew (cartoonbrew.com)

CARTOONSThe Smurfs Get 52 New Episodes, Season 4

Animation World Network reports that season four of The Smurfs is now in production, with fifty-two new episodes. Each new episode runs eleven minutes. Half of them arrive at the end of 2027. The rest come in 2028. 2028 marks the one hundredth birthday of Smurfs creator Peyo, and the seventieth birthday of the Smurfs.

Source: Animation World Network (awn.com)

ANIMENaruto x Ninja Turtles Manga Hits Shelves

Naruto and the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles, together in one manga? Anime News Network reports it ships in North America this week. It comes from a big list of North American anime and manga releases. The full name is Naruto and Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles, Manga Style Edition. Just hearing those two names side by side makes us smile.

Source: Anime News Network (animenewsnetwork.com)

ANIMEMashle Is Back for Season 3 in January

Can muscles beat magic one more time? Animation World Network reports that Mashle Magic and Muscles season three starts streaming on Crunchyroll in January 2027. Mash is the one boy in a world of wizards and witches with no magic at all. Once he's discovered, he's sent to Magic School. There, he sets out to become a Divine Visionary, the elite of the elite.

Official trailer from Crunchyroll on YouTube
Source: Animation World Network (awn.com)

MOVIESSanrio's Kuromi Gets Her First Movie in 2027

Sanrio's Kuromi is starring in her very first movie. Anime News Network reports the distributor GAGA will open it in Japan in 2027. Sanrio shared the news in a video message from Kuromi, with English subtitles. It came out on Halloween, which also happens to be her birthday. The movie's website points to a simple title, Kuromi the Movie.

Source: Anime News Network (animenewsnetwork.com)

STUDIOSAnime Market Grows 15.8% to 4.45 Trillion Yen

Anime News Network reports the anime market hit a record four point four five trillion yen in 2025. That's growth of fifteen point eight percent. The market inside Japan came to one point nine one trillion yen, about twelve point one billion dollars. The overseas market was even bigger, at two point five four trillion yen. The home market rose fourteen point one percent.

Source: Anime News Network (animenewsnetwork.com)

CARTOONSEurope's Emile Animation Awards Winners

Europe just crowned its best animated films of the year! Animation World Network reports that Living Large and Dog Alone took the Best of the Best prizes at the 2026 Emile Awards. Living Large is a feature film by Kristina Dufková. Dog Alone is a short film by Marta Reis Andrade. The awards were presented this past Wednesday night in Krakow, Poland.

Source: Animation World Network (awn.com)

ANIMEShiba Inu Rooms Anime Coming Summer 2027

Ready for some puppy news? Anime News Network reports that Esu Omori's manga Shiba Inu Rooms is getting a television anime! It debuts in summer 2027. Wright Film made the announcement on a Sunday, because November 1 was Wan Wan Wan Day. In Japanese, wan is the sound of a dog barking.

Official trailer from TMSアニメ公式チャンネル on YouTube
Source: Anime News Network (animenewsnetwork.com) · Crunchyroll News (crunchyroll.com/news)

GAMESFinal Fantasy XIV Evercold Dated for Feb 19

Final Fantasy Fourteen's next expansion, Evercold, finally has a date. Anime News Network reports early access starts February 19. The news came from the Fan Festival in Tokyo on Saturday. The full launch follows on February 23, and pre-orders open November 4. This date is a little later than planned.
