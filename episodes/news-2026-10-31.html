CARTOONSThe Smurfs Get 52 New Episodes in Season 4

The Smurfs are getting fifty-two brand new episodes. Animation World Network reports Season four is now in production at Peyo Productions. Each new episode runs eleven minutes. They'll arrive in two parts. Half land at the end of 2027, and the rest follow in 2028.

Source: Animation World Network (awn.com)

MOVIESSanrio's Kuromi Gets Her First Movie in 2027

Sanrio's Kuromi is getting her very first movie. Anime News Network reports it opens in Japan next year, in 2027. Sanrio shared the news on Halloween, which also happens to be Kuromi's birthday. The announcement came as a video message from Kuromi herself, with English subtitles. The movie's website points to a title.

Source: Anime News Network (animenewsnetwork.com)

ANIMEMashle Season 3 Arrives January 2027

Can muscles beat magic all over again? Animation World Network reports that Mashle: Magic and Muscles is getting a third season in January 2027. The new season has a mighty long subtitle. It's called The Tri-Magicathlon Divine Visionary Final Exam Arc. The official trailer is out now.

Official trailer from Crunchyroll on YouTube
Source: Animation World Network (awn.com)

ANIMENaruto x Ninja Turtles Manga Hits Shelves

Naruto and the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles, together in one manga title? It's shipping in North America this week. Anime News Network reports it on its list of North American releases for October 25 through thirty-first. The full title is Naruto x Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles, Manga Style Edition. Those are two big names sharing one title.

Source: Anime News Network (animenewsnetwork.com)

ANIMETwisted-Wonderland Season 2 Starts Dec 23

When does Disney Twisted-Wonderland season two arrive? Anime News Network reports Episode of Savanaclaw starts streaming on Disney Plus on December 23. Disney Plus shared the news on Thursday. The new season will have eight episodes. Disney also unveiled a brand new visual for fans to enjoy.

Source: Anime News Network (animenewsnetwork.com) · Crunchyroll News (crunchyroll.com/news)

MOVIESChiikawa Movie Climbs Back to #2 in Japan

Here's a real comeback story! Chiikawa the Movie, The Secret of Mermaid Island, just climbed from seventh place back up to second in Japan. And that's in its fourteenth weekend. Anime News Network reports the film sold one hundred eighty-five thousand tickets from Friday to Sunday. That earned about one point seven seven million US dollars.

Source: Anime News Network (animenewsnetwork.com)

STUDIOSAnime Market Grows 15.8% to 4.45 Trillion Yen

Anime News Network reports the anime market hit a record four point four five trillion yen in 2025. That's fifteen point eight percent growth. About one point nine one trillion yen came from Japan itself. About two point five four trillion yen came from overseas. That's roughly sixteen point one billion dollars.

Source: Anime News Network (animenewsnetwork.com)

MOVIESLiving Large, Dog Alone Win Emile Awards

A small film just beat some giants. Living Large won the top feature prize at the 2026 Emile Awards, according to Cartoon Brew. The Emiles celebrate the year's best European animation. This was the fifth ceremony. It took place on October 28 in Krakow, Poland, during the Etiuda and Anima International Film Festival.

Source: Cartoon Brew (cartoonbrew.com) · Animation World Network (awn.com)

CARTOONSElmo Gives Peppa a Sesame Street Tour

What happens when Peppa and George visit Sesame Street? Animation World Network reports the final crossover video, Sounds of Sesame Street, is out now. Hasbro and Sesame Workshop made three videos together. It's the very first YouTube team-up between Peppa Pig and Sesame Street. And you can watch it on both shows' channels.

Source: Animation World Network (awn.com)
