Clearly Drawn News: media kit

Clearly Drawn News (CDN) is a daily, all-ages animated news show about anime, cartoons and the animation

industry. Anchor Marcus reports the day's top stories from the CDN desk; in Cartoon Kitchen, Chef Elena and Marcus

recreate famous cartoon and anime dishes from real, credited recipes, with kitchen-safety guidance for families.

  • Format: one 15-20 minute episode every day (9-12 stories with chapters) plus 4 Shorts; Instagram Reels and

TikTok clips. 2K (2560x1440) on YouTube.

  • Audience: families and fans of all ages; content is kid-safe by policy.
  • Coverage: studio and streamer announcements (release dates, seasons, casting, trailers, box office, events),

anime from Japan (translated from Japanese sources), Western animation, animated film, animation games and retro.

  • Sourcing: every story is sourced from named outlets and official announcements, credited on screen and in the

description; press images are credited to their source.

  • Started: October 2026. Publishing: daily.
  • Channels: YouTube @ClearlyDrawnNews · Instagram @therealfocogaming · TikTok @clearlydrawnlessons
  • Press contact: a dedicated press email is being set up; until then, reach us through our YouTube channel.
  • Assets: logo, banner and character art in social_kit/.

What we can do for a release

Same-day coverage of announcements and embargoed news (embargoes honoured to the minute), a dedicated Short for a

trailer or key art, a Cartoon Kitchen episode for a food moment from your title, and on-screen + description credit.
