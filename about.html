About Clearly Drawn News

Clearly Drawn News (CDN) is a daily, all-ages animated news show about anime, cartoons and the animation industry. Anchor Marcus reports the top stories from the CDN desk; in Cartoon Kitchen, Chef Elena and Marcus cook famous cartoon and anime food from real, credited recipes.

Every story is sourced from named outlets and official announcements and credited on screen and here. Japanese-language news is translated by CDN. Our animation, voices and dish art are made with AI-assisted tools; the team chooses every story and checks every script.
