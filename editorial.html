Clearly Drawn News: editorial policy

  • Sourcing. Every story comes from a named outlet or an official announcement. We state only what the source

says; claims not in a source are removed before air by a fact-check step. Sources and links are listed in every

episode description, and the outlet is credited on screen.

  • Translation. Japanese-language reporting is translated into English and credited "translated from Japanese".

No untranslated text goes on air.

  • Images. Press images are shown for news reporting, credited to the publishing outlet, never edited to mislead.

We never redraw or imitate a show's characters in our own art.

  • Embargoes. Embargoed material is scheduled to publish after the embargo lifts; we never publish early.
  • Corrections. Errors are corrected in the description and a pinned comment within 24 hours of being reported;

significant errors are corrected on air in the next episode.

  • All ages. No violence, no politics, nothing frightening; recipes carry grown-up-help and food-safety guidance

(USDA safe temperatures, allergens).

  • AI disclosure. Our animation, voices and dish art are made with AI-assisted tools; the team chooses every

story and checks every script. Platform AI labels are always switched on.

  • Credit. Recipe creators, outlets and image sources are credited on screen and in descriptions, every time.
